What's different in Veo 3.1?

      
Veo 3.1 (released November 2025) improved audiovisual quality, prompt adherence and narrative control compared with Veo 3. In practice, that means two things for your prompts: audio descriptions matter more — native ambient sound and dialogue land closer to what you write — and clear scene structure pays off, because the model follows multi-part instructions more reliably.

      
This generator leans into both: the Audio field maps to 3.1's richer sound controls, and every output is a single structured sentence the model can parse cleanly. If you're still on Veo 3, the Veo 3 prompt generator uses the same structure — the prompts work on both versions.
