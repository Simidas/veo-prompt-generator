Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

The short version

VeoPromptGenerator has no accounts, no forms and no tracking cookies. The generator runs entirely in your browser — the prompts you build never leave your device. We count anonymous, aggregate pageviews with cookieless analytics; details below.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email addresses.
  • No prompts, scenes or generated content are sent to or stored on our servers.
  • No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • No cookies or identifiers of any kind for analytics.

Analytics

We measure overall traffic with Plausible Analytics, a cookieless, privacy-friendly analytics tool hosted on our own infrastructure. Plausible does not use cookies or localStorage, does not identify individual visitors and does not track you across other websites. It stores only aggregate, non-identifying information such as page URL, referrer, browser, device type and approximate country.

What is logged automatically

This site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes standard technical request data (such as IP address and requested URL) to serve pages and protect against abuse. We don't use this data to profile you.

Changes

If we add features that change how data is handled (for example optional accounts or payments), we will update this policy and note the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/Simidas/veo-prompt-generator/issues.
