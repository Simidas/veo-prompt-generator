About VeoPromptGenerator

We make prompt tools for creators who'd rather shoot than type.

VeoPromptGenerator started from a simple frustration: video generation credits are expensive, and most of them get burned on prompts that were never going to work. Camera left to chance, audio undescribed, three scenes crammed into one.

So we built the tool we wanted: a free prompt generator that walks you through the decisions a film crew would make — camera, lighting, audio, style — with a plain-English tip on every option, so you learn the vocabulary while you build. No sign-up, no credit card, unlimited template prompts.

It's an independent project. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google or DeepMind. "Veo" is a trademark of Google; we use the name only to describe which models our prompts target.
